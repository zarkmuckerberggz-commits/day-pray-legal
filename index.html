📖 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19th September 2026

Who We Are

Day Pray is a faith-based prayer app designed to help users pray more consistently. Day Pray is developed and operated by ZMPU Production. Contact: zarkmuckerberggz@gmail.com

What Data We Collect

  • Your first name (entered during onboarding)
  • Your selected objectives and commitment level
  • Journal entries you write within the app
  • Feedback messages you choose to send us
  • Your prayer streak and session history
  • Verses, reflections, and prayers you save to Favourites
  • Your preferred notification time (if enabled)
  • Basic usage and app-analytics data (see "Analytics" below)
  • Subscription status (active, trial, expired) via our subscription management provider

We do not collect precise location data, advertising IDs, or your payment card details. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or bank information.

Analytics

We use PostHog for basic product analytics (for example, which features are used). PostHog may process your IP address as part of standard web analytics, which can indicate an approximate general location (such as city or country level). We do not use this to identify you individually or to track your precise, real-world location.

How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely in Firebase (Google) cloud servers, tied to an anonymous account identity created automatically when you first use the app. Because Day Pray does not currently use a sign-in system, data recovery after reinstalling the app or switching devices is not guaranteed — if this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Feedback messages are sent via EmailJS to our email inbox.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you uninstall the app without requesting deletion, your data remains stored on our servers in case you reinstall and continue using Day Pray later. You may request full deletion of your data at any time — see "Your Rights" below.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Day Pray experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for their own marketing purposes.

AI-Generated Content

Day Pray uses Groq to generate personalised reflections and prayers. Only your selected emotion/need and the corresponding verse are sent to this service. Your name, journal entries, and other personal data are never shared with Groq.

Age Requirement & Children's Privacy

Day Pray is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a user under 18 — including a child under 13 — we will delete it promptly.

How to Delete Your Data

Day Pray, developed by ZMPU Production, gives every user the ability to request full deletion of their data at any time.

Steps to request deletion:

  • Email zarkmuckerberggz@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  • Include the name you entered in the app so we can locate your account (we do not use email/username accounts, so this helps us identify the correct data)
  • We will confirm and process your request within 30 days (faster where required by law — see "Your Rights" below)

What gets deleted: your name, journal entries, favourites, selected objectives, commitment level, prayer streak and session history, notification preferences, and subscription status records tied to your account.

What may be retained: anonymized or aggregated analytics data that cannot be tied back to you individually (via PostHog), and any records Google Play/RevenueCat retain independently for billing, tax, or legal compliance purposes, which are outside our direct control.

Your Rights

Users in Malaysia: Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), you may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Deletion requests will be processed within 30 days.

Users in the European Economic Area or UK: You have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and the right to object to certain processing.

Users in California: You have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected, and the right to request deletion.

Wherever you are located, you can exercise these rights by contacting us at zarkmuckerberggz@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, and no later than required by applicable law.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — data storage and anonymous authentication
  • EmailJS — feedback transmission
  • PostHog — product analytics
  • Groq — AI reflection generation
  • RevenueCat — subscription status management
  • Google Play — subscription billing and payment processing

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
